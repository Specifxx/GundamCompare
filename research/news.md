## Next set: GD06 Stardust Trails
- **Release: 2026-10-30.** MSRP US$4.99 / £3.75 per 12-card pack. Themes: *Gundam ZZ* (40th anniversary) and *0083 Stardust Memory* (35th). Source: https://www.gundam-gcg.com/en/products/gd06.html , https://gundamlab.com/blogs/news/gundam-card-game-stardust-trails-gd06-release-guide
- Rarities (official): 50 C / 36 U / 32 R / 12 LR / 5 Special / 8 tokens. TCGplayer currently lists 142 products: 58 C, 36 U, 32 R, 12 LR plus sealed.
- Legend Rares include ZZ Gundam, Gundam GP01Fb, Infinite Justice Gundam, GQuuuuuuX (Omega Psycommu Released), Elpeo Ple's Qubeley Mk-II and Turn X (TCGCSV group 24804). The expected chases are the ZZ Gundam and GP01Fb LR+ and LR++ parallels.

GundamLAB's first allocation sold out, and GameStop limits packs per account. ### Parallel/special rarity ladder and price levels (the GD05 benchmark)
Every rarity has a "+" parallel (C+, U+, R+, LR+). LR++ is the top alt-art. There are also SP (special) cards and SP-foil EX Resource tokens. GD05 top market prices on TCGplayer, from TCGCSV on 2026-10-24:
- Wing Gundam Zero (EW) LR++: **$3,910**; Strike Freedom LR++ $2,606; Nu Gundam LR++ $1,532; Sazabi LR++ $1,273; Master Gundam LR++ $1,113
- EX Resource SP tokens: $225–$768 (EXR-009 $768)
- GD05 booster case: $2,513
- Earlier set: GD01-001 Gundam sells for about $2 as a base card, about $48 as LR+ and about $590 as LR++ (https://www.pricecharting.com/game/gundam-newtype-rising/gundam-gd01-001)

## Hot topics
- **Banned & Restricted list:** the first list took effect 2026-04-01 and restricted Corsica Base (ST02-016) to 2 copies. The 2026-07-24 revision brought the **first ban, Anksha (GD01-020)**, which Bandai tied to Blue/Purple dominance. It also introduced **banned pairs** such as Amuro Ray + Mikazuki Augus and Guntank + M1 Astray Shrike. The official list was updated again on 2026-09-25, and Bandai revises it roughly every six months. Sources: https://www.gundam-gcg.com/en/news/01_279.html , https://www.gundam-gcg.com/en/news/01_277.html , https://www.tcgplayer.com/content/article/The-Gundam-Card-Game-s-First-Bans-and-Bigger-Restrictions/efab1480-3657-4f78-893f-2709fe9be5d8/ , https://cardsoncardsoncards.com.au/blog/gundam-card-game-ban-list-july-2026/
- **Organized play:** the 2026–27 World Championship Regionals are running, and Double Infinity sells a "World Championship Regionals 26-27" promo. Release events and ST09 event promos (RP/EXBP) are also running. I found no World Championship results or ruleset; those need follow-up **(unverified)**. Floor rules: https://www.gundam-gcg.com/asia-en/pdf/floor_rule_asia-en.pdf
- **Price spikes:** GD05 LR++ cards in the $1k–$4k range and SP EX Resource tokens are the big movers. GD06 box pre-orders are selling out. Japanese and Asia-English boxes are sold alongside English ones (Gameology, GundamLAB JPN).
- **Gundam Assemble** (a Bandai miniatures game, ST01A/ST02A "Gundam Assemble Starter Sets") is mixed into many retailers' Gundam collections. Filter it out when scraping.
